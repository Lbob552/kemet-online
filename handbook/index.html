The Story

Ra and Apophis fought a war beneath the world, in the Duat. The battle tore a rift under the Nile, and through it bled Isfet, the chaos that Ma'at, the order of the world, was made to hold back. The river turned to poisoned sludge. The gods of Egypt, flooded with Isfet, became mad tyrants, and their temples became strongholds of monster hordes.

You are one of the last free warriors of Kemet. From Abu at the First Cataract you travel north, region by region, freeing each god and winning back the 7 fragments of the Divine Ankh from Set, Amun, Osiris, Sobek, Thoth, Ptah and Ra. With all of them, you can face Apophis himself in the mountains of Sinai and seal the gate of the Underworld.

There is no coinage in Kemet: trade runs on deben, weighed copper, silver and gold.

The World of Kemet

The road runs south to north along the Nile, through the great cities of Egypt. Each region has its own people, monsters, quests and a god to free.

The Gods & their Heka Cards

Every god you meet, free or defeat can grant their Heka Card: 56 in all. Wearable cards are worn like gear, Aura cards give a blessing for 4 minutes, and Summon cards let you take a god's form: ×3.0 health, ×2.0 attack.

Trials of the Gods

Hidden challenges given by the gods, unlocked from level 30: Trials of the Lesser Gods (level 30), Trials of the Temple Gods (level 42), Trials of the Ennead (level 50). Each rewards a god's card.

  • The Dew Jars Trials of the Lesser Gods

    Hapi's floods remember Tefnut, who is rain and dew. Six sacred jars of her dew were lost among the orchards of Shedet, in the Faiyum - gather them.

    Given by Hapi · reward: Tefnut's card + 3,000 deben
  • The Feathers of Shu Trials of the Lesser Gods

    Atum's first-born, Shu, is the breath between earth and sky. Three of his feathers drifted down on the three cities of the middle road - find them all.

    Given by Atum · reward: Shu's card + 3,000 deben
  • The Hippo Amulets Trials of the Lesser Gods

    Bes guards the home, and Taweret guards the mother. Her amulets are swallowed by the Plague Crocodiles of Lake Moeris, in Shedet - take three from them.

    Given by Bes · reward: Taweret's card + 3,000 deben
  • The Sting of Serket Trials of the Lesser Gods

    Neith's companion Serket rules the sting and the cure. Take two stingers from the Giant Desert Scorpions of Abydos to earn her trust.

    Given by Neith · reward: Serket's card + 3,000 deben
  • The Uraeus Fragments Trials of the Lesser Gods

    Renenutet the cobra-mother tells of Wadjet, the green cobra of the crown. Three fragments of her uraeus lie in three lands - find them.

    Given by Renenutet · reward: Wadjet's card + 3,000 deben
  • The Vulture's Feathers Trials of the Lesser Gods

    Seshat keeps the record of the Two Ladies of the crown. Nekhbet's feathers are carried off by the Ibis Horrors of Khemenu - retrieve three.

    Given by Seshat · reward: Nekhbet's card + 3,000 deben
  • Nine Lives Trials of the Temple Gods

    At Iunu, Bastet was the Great Cat under the Ished tree who cut off Apophis's head with a knife. Sekhmet's gentler sister wants the serpent's spawn gone from Ra's city - put nine to rest and she will come.

    Given by Sekhmet · reward: Bastet's card + 6,000 deben
  • The Four Words of Power Trials of the Temple Gods

    Four words of power were spoken at the beginning and then hidden across the land - one on Philae, one in the Pyramid Desert, one on the Nile Banks and one at Edfu. Find all four and the hidden god will answer.

    Given by Heka · reward: Khonsu's card + 6,000 deben
  • The Mother's Riddles Trials of the Temple Gods

    Isis sends you to Mut, the great mother, who tests every child of the gods with five riddles. Answer them true.

    Given by Isis · reward: Mut's card + 6,000 deben
  • The Rays of the Disk Trials of the Temple Gods

    Khepri whispers of a sun older than Ra, a single disc with hands for rays. The Scarabs of the Dusk at Iunu hide its light under their shells - defeat eighteen to draw it out.

    Given by Khepri · reward: Aten's card + 6,000 deben
  • The Spears of Montu Trials of the Temple Gods

    Anhur speaks of Montu, the falcon of war whom Thebes loved before Amun. His spears were turned to stone at Edfu - break fifteen Stone Guardians to free them.

    Given by Anhur · reward: Montu's card + 6,000 deben
  • The Archive's Nine Riddles Trials of the Ennead

    Nine riddles from the archive of the gods. Answer all nine in a row - one wrong answer and the archive closes its doors for a while, and the riddles change.

    Given by Sia · reward: Nephthys's card + 12,000 deben
  • The Riddles of the Sky Trials of the Ennead

    Geb's wife Nut will only speak to one who has looked up. Answer five riddles of the heavens; a wrong answer closes the sky for a while.

    Given by Geb · reward: Nut's card + 12,000 deben
  • The Weighing of the Heart Trials of the Ennead

    Maat weighs hearts against her feather, and Ammit waits below the scales. Answer seven riddles of judgment without one error - she does not forgive a wrong answer for long.

    Given by Maat · reward: Ammit's card + 12,000 deben

How to Play

Online

Sign in with Google in the game (Play Online). Your account has three character slots, each with its own journey, saved to your account. Players in the same region share the world: you see each other and fight the same monsters. Everyone who strikes a monster shares the kill, and each player gets their own loot.

Offline

The whole story can also be played alone, without an account. Offline progress stays on your device and is separate from your online characters.

Heka Cards

Collect cards from quests, bosses and trials. Wear them, raise auras, or summon a god and fight in their form. Gods level up as you use them.

Your look

Make your hero in a 3D creator: skin tone and undertone, freckles, three face shapes, seven eye colours, eyebrows, kohl and war paint, men's and women's hairstyles with their own colours, a beard for men, six cloth sets with 13 dyes, height and build. The Waset mirror lets you change it later.

Blacksmith

Karem the Smith in Waset raises plate armour a rank (Bronze, Iron, Dark Steel, Gilded, Mithril) with Smith's Ingots and deben, tempers weapons one rarity step (common, unique, epic, super) and forges ingots from deben. Wear all seven plate pieces and the set counts as its lowest rank: +4% health and attack per rank.

Parties

Up to five players. A kill counts for every member near it, and each extra member sharing a kill adds +10% XP for all of you (up to +40%). Loot is personal: everyone rolls their own.

Languages

English and Arabic. Switch with the language button in the top-right corner of the title screen; the choice is remembered.

Death

Without Khnum's protection the fall was final. You keep your level, but the experience toward your next level and everything in your stash are lost.
