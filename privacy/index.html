Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what information Sands of Kemet ("the game", "we") collects when you play online, why, and what you can do about it. If you only play offline, the game collects nothing: your progress stays on your device.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID. We do not receive your Google password, contacts, files or anything else from your Google account.
  • Your game data. Your characters (names, heroes, levels) and their progress: quests, items, Heka Cards and similar game state.
  • Chat and reports. Messages you send in the game's chat are passed to other players. A message is stored only when a player reports it, so it can be reviewed.
  • Technical data. Like any online service, our servers see your IP address and basic connection details while you are connected. This is used to run the game and to stop abuse.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your characters and progress saved to your account.
  • To run the shared online world (showing your character to other players, parties, chat, trading).
  • To keep the game fair and safe: handling reports, muting or banning accounts that break the rules.

We do not sell your information, show you ads, or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Where it is stored

Accounts and game data are stored with Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, Germany). Sign-in is provided by Google. Game downloads are served by GitHub. These providers process data only to provide their service.

How long we keep it

Your account and characters are kept while your account exists. A deleted character is kept for up to 30 days so it can be restored if deleted by mistake, then removed. Reported chat messages are kept up to 90 days.

Your choices

  • You can delete any of your characters inside the game at any time.
  • To delete your whole account and all its data, or to get a copy of your data, email ehabnaga1@gmail.com from the Google address you sign in with. We will do it within 30 days.
  • You can remove the game's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Online play is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
