Download the game

Sands of Kemet is on Android. Download the APK on your phone or tablet and open it to install. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.

Download for Android

version 1.0.2 · 74 MB. The download is small. On first launch the game fetches the rest of Kemet, so start it on Wi-Fi.

Step 3 of 3

Play

  1. Open Sands of Kemet.
  2. Tap Play Online and sign in with the same Google account.
  3. Create your character, choose a hero, and begin at Abu, by the First Cataract.

Playing offline instead? Tap Play Offline: the whole story is there, saved on your device.
