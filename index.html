Sands of Kemet

An action RPG through ancient Egypt, for Android

Journey from Khnum's shrine at the First Cataract to the turquoise mines of Sinai. Fight the creatures of Isfet, meet the gods of Egypt, collect Heka Cards, and restore Ma'at to the Two Lands.

Play alone or online

Play the whole story offline, or sign in with your Google account to play online in a shared world with other players: form parties, chat, trade and duel. Each account has three character slots, each with its own journey.

Signing in

Online play uses Google Sign-In only to identify your account. The game's sign-in page lives at this site's sign-in page and is opened by the game itself.
