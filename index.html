The Road North

From the First Cataract to the mountains of Sinai: nine regions along the Nile, each held by a god you have to free. ☥ marks a fragment of the Ankh.

  1. I · ☥

    Abu Elephantine

    Where the river enters Egypt. Khnum's shrine above the cataract, Isis's island under a storm.

  2. II

    Djeba Apollonopolis Magna

    Horus's great temple, held by a shadow of Set.

  3. III · ☥

    Waset Thebes

    Thebes of the hundred gates. Its hidden king has turned on his own city.

  4. IV · ☥

    Abdju Abydos

    Osiris lies torn apart in his own sanctuary. Make him whole.

  5. V · ☥

    Shedet Crocodilopolis

    The Faiyum's lake has gone green and rotten, and its crocodile lord with it.

  6. VI · ☥

    Khemenu Hermopolis Magna

    City of the Eight, where Thoth's wisdom has curdled into rage.

  7. VII · ☥

    Men-nefer Memphis

    Behind the White Walls, Ptah's colossus walks.

  8. VIII · ☥

    Iunu Heliopolis

    The city of the sun. Ra's own soul burns as the Bennu bird.

  9. IX

    Mefkat Sinai

    The turquoise mountains. The serpent waits for the Ankh.

  10. Beneath the road

    Rostau the Duat

    A hidden door in Waset opens on the Duat, where Anubis keeps the scales.

What You Become

Choose a hero

8 heroes, from the Medjay warrior with sword and shield to the desert hunter and the long bow.

Carry the gods

Earn 56 Heka Cards from quests, bosses and trials. Wear them like gear, or raise a god's aura around you.

Become a god

Summon a god and fight in their form, with ×3.0 health and ×2.0 attack. The gods grow stronger the more you use them.

Walk it together

Friends in the same region share the world and the fight. Everyone who strikes shares the kill, and each player gets their own loot. You can also play the whole story offline.
